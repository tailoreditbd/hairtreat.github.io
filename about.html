About HairTreat

HairTreat is a doctor-led hair transplant and hair restoration clinic in Uttara, Dhaka, with a second chamber in Tangail. We help men and women deal with hair loss through hair transplant, PRP therapy, and laser treatments, and every plan begins with a proper medical assessment rather than a sales pitch.

Hair loss is personal. Some patients want a lower hairline back, some want thicker density, and others want a fuller beard or eyebrows. Our job is to explain your options honestly, plan the treatment around your scalp and donor hair, and support you through recovery.

Our approach to hair restoration

We believe good results come from careful planning, not from a one-size-fits-all package. That belief shapes how we work with every patient:

Assessment first: We check your hair-loss pattern, scalp condition, donor area, and medical history before suggesting any procedure.

Honest advice: If a patient is not a good candidate, or would do better with PRP or another non-surgical option, we say so.

Natural design: Hairlines, density, and graft direction are planned to suit your face, age, and future hair loss.

Clear costs: You receive a personal estimate after your consultation, so the graft count and total cost are clear before you decide.

Support after treatment: Recovery guidance and follow-up visits are part of the process, not an afterthought.

Treatments we offer

At HairTreat, we offer a full range of hair restoration treatments for men and women, from hair transplant to PRP and laser care. Each treatment is recommended only after a proper consultation, so you get the option that truly suits your hair and goals.

Hair transplant

  • Hair transplant: permanent restoration using your own follicles
  • Female hair transplant: density and hairline restoration designed for women
  • Hairline correction: reshaping an uneven or receding hairline
  • Beard transplant: filling patchy or thin facial hair
  • Eyebrow transplant: fuller, natural-looking brows

Other treatments

  • Hair PRP and GFC PRP: growth-factor therapy to support weak follicles
  • Laser hair removal: long-lasting hair reduction
  • P-Shot: a PRP-based regenerative treatment

Understanding treatment cost

The cost of a hair treatment depends on your situation, not on a fixed price list. After a short consultation, we give you a personal estimate with no obligation. You can also read our general pricing guide. The main factors are:

  • Your hair and scalp condition: Mild thinning may need a smaller plan than advanced hair loss.
  • The treatment type: A transplant, a PRP course, and laser sessions are priced differently, and some need several sessions.
  • The number of grafts or sessions: Larger areas take more time and more follicles.
  • The clinic's expertise and follow-up care: Experienced doctors, safe technology, and proper aftercare are part of what you pay for.
