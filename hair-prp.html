What is hair PRP?

Hair PRP, or platelet-rich plasma therapy, is a non-surgical hair restoration procedure. A small amount of the patient's blood is processed to separate platelet-rich plasma, which is then injected into selected areas of the scalp. PRP generally works best when follicles are still present but have become weak. It cannot create new follicles in completely bald areas where the hair roots are no longer active. A medical consultation is therefore important before beginning treatment. Key features of hair PRP treatment include:

  • It uses platelet-rich plasma prepared from the patient's own blood.
  • It is a non-surgical procedure with no follicle extraction or implantation.
  • PRP may help strengthen weak and miniaturized hair follicles.
  • It can support healthier-looking hair density in suitable patients.
  • The procedure may help reduce excessive hair shedding over time.
  • It usually involves minimal downtime compared with surgical treatment.
  • PRP may be used as a standalone treatment or alongside a hair transplant.
  • Treatment sessions can be personalized according to the level of hair loss.
  • Both men and women may be considered for PRP after proper assessment.
  • Results develop gradually and may vary from one patient to another.

HairTreat step-by-step process for hair PRP

HairTreat follows a structured PRP process to maintain treatment safety, patient comfort, and consistent application. The number of sessions and treatment intervals are decided after reviewing the scalp and hair-loss pattern. The exact schedule depends on the patient’s response, cause of hair loss, and overall condition. Here is our step-by-step process:

Step Treatment Stage What Happens
1

Consultation and scalp assessment

The doctor reviews the patient’s hair-loss history, scalp condition, existing density, medical background, and treatment expectations.

2

Blood collection

A small amount of blood is collected from the patient using a sterile medical procedure.

3

PRP preparation

The blood is placed in a centrifuge to separate platelet-rich plasma from the other blood components.

4

Scalp preparation

The targeted areas are cleaned and prepared. A numbing method may be used when appropriate to reduce discomfort.

5

PRP application

The prepared PRP is carefully injected into selected thinning areas of the scalp using controlled placement.

6

Aftercare guidance

The patient receives instructions about scalp care, washing, exercise, medication, and the next scheduled session.

Average costs of hair PRP in Bangladesh

The average cost of hair PRP in Bangladesh varies between clinics because the treatment method, number of sessions, and quality of materials may differ. An exact price should be confirmed after a scalp consultation and personalized treatment assessment. Here are some key points:

A single PRP session generally costs less than a surgical hair transplant.

The total cost increases when several initial sessions are recommended.

Maintenance sessions may be needed to support the achieved improvement.

Advanced PRP preparation systems may affect the treatment price.

Scalp examination, medicines, or additional hair treatments may be charged separately.

Combination packages with PRP and hair transplant aftercare may have different pricing.

Patients should consider hygiene, medical supervision, and treatment quality rather than selecting a clinic based only on the lowest cost.
