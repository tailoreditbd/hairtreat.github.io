Cost Factors

What affects hair transplant cost?

Hair transplant cost depends on several factors, including the number of grafts needed, the technique used, and your hair loss condition. Understanding these factors can help you estimate the cost and choose a suitable treatment plan.

  • Number of grafts: more bald area means more grafts and a higher total.
  • Hair loss stage and pattern: early thinning and advanced baldness need different plans.
  • Donor area strength: the density and quality of your donor hair set how many grafts can be safely harvested.
  • Technique: normal FUE, Sapphire FUE, and DHI are priced differently.
  • Package and aftercare: PRP sessions, medicines, and follow-ups change what your package includes.

FUE vs. Sapphire FUE vs. DHI: Cost comparison

The cost of a hair transplant in Bangladesh varies depending on the technique used. Normal FUE is generally the most affordable option, while Sapphire FUE costs more because it uses sapphire blades to create recipient channels. DHI typically has the highest per-graft price, as it uses a specialised implantation technique.

Each method has its own advantages, but the right choice depends on your hair loss pattern, donor hair condition, graft requirements and treatment goals. At HairTreat, your doctor will assess these factors before recommending a suitable technique.
