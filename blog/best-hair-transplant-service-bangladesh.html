How We Selected These Hair Transplant Clinics

There is no independent national ranking that can objectively declare one hair transplant clinic the best for every patient. Our list therefore focuses on practical factors patients should evaluate before choosing a provider.

Most importantly, patients should determine exactly who performs the medical and surgical stages of the procedure. The ISHRS recommends that appropriately trained and licensed physicians be responsible for diagnosis, surgical planning, donor harvesting, hairline design, recipient-site creation, and management of medical issues associated with hair restoration surgery.

The main criteria include:

  • Availability of qualified medical professionals
  • Focus on hair restoration
  • Hair loss and scalp assessment
  • Donor-area evaluation
  • Hairline planning
  • Availability of established transplant techniques
  • Clear communication about graft requirements
  • Availability of aftercare
  • Published information about procedures
  • Accessibility for Bangladeshi patients

Hair transplant clinic বেছে নেওয়ার আগে যে ৭টি বিষয় দেখবেন

A beautiful website, discount package, or high graft number should not be the main reason for selecting a clinic.

Before paying a deposit, ask a few important questions.

Who will perform my hair transplant?: Find out the name and qualifications of the doctor responsible for your procedure. Also ask which stages are performed directly by the doctor and which involve assistants.

Why am I losing hair?: Hair transplant surgery does not treat every form of hair loss. A dermatologist or appropriately trained medical professional should first determine the likely cause. The American Academy of Dermatology notes that dermatologists can distinguish different types of hair loss and determine whether transplantation may be appropriate.

How healthy is my donor area?: Your donor area determines how many grafts can reasonably be harvested. A responsible treatment plan should consider donor density, hair characteristics, the size of the recipient area and possible future hair loss rather than promising an arbitrary number of grafts.

What will my hairline look like in the future?: A very low hairline can look attractive immediately but may not remain appropriate as the patient ages or experiences further hair loss. Ask why the proposed hairline has been selected and how grafts will be distributed between the frontal area, temples and crown.

Which technique is suitable for me?: FUE, Sapphire FUE and DHI are not interchangeable marketing packages. The appropriate technique depends on factors such as donor condition, graft requirements, hair characteristics, recipient area and the surgical plan.

What happens after surgery?: Aftercare is part of the transplant process. Ask about washing instructions, medication, donor and recipient-area care, follow-up appointments and whom to contact if you experience an unexpected problem.
