How hair growth progresses after a transplant

Hair growth after a transplant happens in stages, so the full result takes time to appear. Here is what you can expect from the first weeks to the final outcome.

  • Weeks 2–4: Transplanted hairs shed. This is normal.
  • Months 3–4: New growth begins.
  • Months 6–8: Visible density improves.
  • Months 12–18: Final, natural-looking result.

What affects your result

Every patient's result is different, and several factors decide how your new hair grows and looks. Here are the main ones that shape your outcome.

  • Your hair type and donor area quality
  • The transplant method used
  • Surgeon skill and planning
  • Following aftercare instructions
