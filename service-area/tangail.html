Hair transplant methods we offer

After your assessment, we'll recommend the technique best suited to your hair loss pattern and donor area:

  • FUE Hair Transplant : Follicles are extracted individually and placed without a linear scar — a common choice for most patients.
  • Sapphire FUE : Uses sapphire blades for finer recipient channels and a more refined hairline.
  • DHI : Direct implantation for greater control over graft angle and density.
  • FUT : A strip technique that can be considered for larger areas needing coverage.

Other treatments available

Not every case needs surgery. Depending on your consultation, we may suggest:

  • Hair PRP and GFC PRP to support thinning hair and scalp health
  • Beard Transplant for patchy beard growth
  • Eyebrow Transplant for sparse brows
  • Female Hair Transplant for diffuse thinning
  • Hairline Correction for an uneven or previously corrected hairline

Treatment Process

How the process works for our patients

Every treatment starts with understanding your hair loss before recommending a solution. Every patient heals differently, and we'll always explain what's realistic for your specific case.

  1. Local consultation: Meet our team at the Tangail chamber to discuss your hair loss and history.
  2. Assessment and plan: Receive a recommended technique, estimated graft count, and hairline design.
  3. Procedure scheduling: If a transplant is needed, the procedure itself takes place at our main facility.
  4. Aftercare guidance: You'll get clear instructions on washing, medication, and recovery.
  5. Follow-up: We track your progress as new growth develops over the following months.

Hair transplant cost for Tangail patients

You'll receive a clear, consultation-based estimate before committing to treatment. See our pricing page for general cost factors. Pricing isn't fixed — it depends on your individual needs:

  • Number of grafts required to cover the thinning or bald area
  • Technique selected (FUE, Sapphire, DHI, or FUT)
  • Extent of hair loss and area to be treated
  • Donor area strength and density
  • Additional care, such as PRP or post-procedure medication
