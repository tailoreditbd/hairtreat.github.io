How it works for Singapore NRBs

Many Bangladeshi expats in Singapore choose HairTreat for the same trusted care they'd get back home, without the wait or the guesswork. Here's how we make the process simple for NRBs planning their visit around treatment.

  1. Message us before you fly. Send your travel dates and a few clear photos of your hairline, top, crown, and donor area on WhatsApp.
  2. Get preliminary guidance. Our team reviews your photos and explains what to expect. The final plan is confirmed only after an in-person exam.
  3. Meet the doctor in Uttara. He checks your scalp, donor density, and hair loss stage, then recommends the method and estimates the grafts you need.
  4. Have the procedure and aftercare briefing. The transplant is done under local anesthesia, and most cases are completed in one sitting. Before you leave, you receive full aftercare instructions and the doctor's advice on when it is safe to fly back.
  5. Time zone tip: Singapore is two hours ahead of Bangladesh. Our appointment desk runs from 11 AM to 8 PM Bangladesh time, which is 1 PM to 10 PM in Singapore.
