When do you need laser hair removal?

You may need laser hair removal when shaving, waxing, or threading becomes painful, repetitive, or causes skin irritation. It is a good option for people who want permanent hair removal of unwanted hair with smoother skin. A consultation helps confirm whether your skin type, hair type, and treatment area are suitable for laser treatment. Here are some points:

  • Unwanted hair on face, arms, legs, underarms, back, chest, or bikini area.
  • Shaving causes cuts, bumps, or dark spots.
  • Waxing or threading feels painful or time-consuming.
  • You often get ingrown hairs or razor bumps.
  • Hair grows back too quickly after shaving.
  • You want smoother skin for longer.
  • You want a cleaner look with less regular maintenance.

Side effects of laser hair removal

Laser hair removal is generally safe when performed by trained professionals using the right laser settings for your skin and hair type. Some mild side effects may appear after treatment, but they are usually temporary and improve with proper aftercare. To reduce side effects, patients should avoid direct sun exposure, follow skincare instructions, and complete laser hair removal only under professional supervision.

Redness and mild swelling around the treated area for a few hours.

Warm or tingling sensation on the skin after the session.

Temporary skin sensitivity, especially when exposed to sunlight.

Mild itching or dryness as the skin adjusts after treatment.

Slight darkening or lightening of skin tone in some cases, especially without sun protection.

Minor irritation or bumps similar to razor bumps, usually short-term.

Rare risk of burns or blisters if the laser setting is not suitable or aftercare is ignored.

FAQs about laser hair removal treatments in Bangladesh

Laser treatment can produce long-term hair reduction, but it does not guarantee that every hair will disappear permanently. Regrowth is usually finer, lighter and less noticeable, while occasional maintenance may be required.

Many patients need approximately six or more sessions. The exact number depends on the treatment area, hair growth cycle, skin tone, hair thickness and hormonal condition.

Most patients describe the sensation as mild warmth, pinching or a rubber-band-like snap. Cooling technology or a suitable numbing method may help improve comfort.

Modern laser systems can treat a wider range of skin tones, but the technology and settings must be selected carefully. An assessment and patch test are particularly important for darker skin.

Shaving is usually allowed because it does not remove the follicle from beneath the skin. Waxing, threading and plucking may need to be avoided because they remove the hair root targeted by the laser.

Avoid direct sunlight, tanning, harsh skincare, excessive heat and activities that irritate the treated area. Follow the specific aftercare instructions provided by your treatment professional.

The cost varies according to the body area, required sessions, technology and treatment package. HairTreat provides a personalised price estimate after assessing the patient’s skin, hair and desired treatment areas.
