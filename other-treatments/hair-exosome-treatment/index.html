What is hair exosome treatment?

Exosomes are microscopic vesicles released by cells. They carry proteins, growth factors, and genetic messengers that help cells communicate. In hair care, exosome products are applied to the scalp, commonly with microneedling or mesotherapy, so these signals can reach the hair follicles.

Unlike a hair transplant, this is a non-surgical option. It does not move hair from one area to another. It aims to support the follicles you already have. Research on exosomes for hair loss is still developing, so results vary, and a consultation is the best way to set realistic expectations.

Who needs hair exosome treatment?

Exosome therapy is generally not suited to areas that are completely bald and have no active follicles. In those cases, our specialist may suggest a different approach. You may benefit from this treatment if:

  • Your hair is thinning, but you still have active follicles.
  • You notice more hair on your pillow, comb, or shower drain.
  • Your parting looks wider than before.
  • Your hairline is receding at an early stage.
  • You want a non-surgical option before considering a transplant.
  • Other treatments have given limited improvement.
  • You want to support the scalp before or after a hair transplant.

Side effects of hair exosome treatment

When performed by trained professionals using sterile, quality-checked products, the treatment is generally well tolerated. Possible side effects are usually temporary:

  • Mild redness on the scalp for a few hours to a day.
  • Tenderness or tingling where the product was applied.
  • Slight swelling around the treated area in some cases.
  • Tiny pinpoint marks if microneedling is used.
  • Temporary itching or dryness as the scalp settles.
  • Rare infection or allergic reaction if hygiene or product suitability is not properly managed.

Aftercare tips for hair exosome

Following these simple aftercare tips after your exosome session helps your scalp heal well and supports the best possible results.

  • Avoid washing your hair for the period your specialist recommends.
  • Stay out of direct sun and use a cap or scarf outdoors.
  • Skip harsh hair products, color, and heat styling until advised.
  • Avoid heavy exercise and sweating for the first day or two.
  • Do not scratch or rub the scalp.
  • Attend your follow-up sessions on schedule.
