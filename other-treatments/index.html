Other treatments at HairTreat

Not every hair or skin concern needs surgery. At HairTreat in Uttara, Dhaka, our doctor-led clinic offers a range of non-surgical treatments that support scalp health, encourage stronger hair, and address everyday grooming needs.

Each one begins with a proper consultation, so your plan fits your goals instead of a fixed package. Browse the treatments below, or book a consultation and let our specialist guide you.

Our non-surgical and specialty treatments

From PRP and exosome therapy to laser hair removal, each treatment below is chosen after a doctor's assessment.

Hair PRP

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy uses a small sample of your own blood. The platelet-rich portion is separated and placed into the scalp to nourish weak follicles. It is a popular choice for early thinning, shedding, or hair that has lost its strength. Many patients also pair it with other care for added support.

GFC PRP

GFC (growth factor concentrate) is a refined form of PRP. It concentrates the growth factors in your blood without the extra blood cells, then delivers them to the scalp to stimulate follicles. If standard PRP is not the right fit, or you want a more targeted option, our doctor can advise during your assessment.

Hair exosome treatment

Exosomes are tiny messenger particles that carry signals between cells. In a hair exosome session, they are applied to the scalp to support the environment around your follicles. It is a newer, regenerative approach for people who want to improve scalp and hair quality without surgery.

Laser hair removal

Laser treatment targets unwanted hair at the root and reduces regrowth over a series of sessions. It suits many body areas and is a lasting alternative to shaving or waxing. We also offer facial hair laser treatment for smoother skin on the face.

P-Shot

The P-Shot is a PRP-based regenerative treatment for men's intimate wellness. It aims to support blood circulation in the treated area. Because this is a private matter, we handle it discreetly and begin with a confidential consultation to check whether it suits you.
