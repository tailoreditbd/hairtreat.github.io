Our hair treatments process

Every treatment starts with understanding your hair loss before recommending a solution. Every patient heals differently, and we'll always explain what's realistic for your specific case.

  1. Local consultation: Meet our team at the Tangail chamber to discuss your hair loss and history.
  2. Assessment and plan: Receive a recommended technique, estimated graft count, and hairline design.
  3. Procedure scheduling: If a transplant is needed, the procedure itself takes place at our main facility.
  4. Aftercare guidance: You'll get clear instructions on washing, medication, and recovery.
  5. Follow-up: We track your progress as new growth develops over the following months.
