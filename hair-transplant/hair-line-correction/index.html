Hairline correction service in Bangladesh | HairTreat

A hairline frames the face. When it sits too high, looks uneven, or has pulled back at the temples, the whole face can look different, and no styling trick fixes it for long. Hairline correction in Bangladesh gives a permanent answer: your own hair follicles are moved from a donor area, usually the back of the scalp, to rebuild a lower, balanced, natural-looking frontal line.

HairTreat offers doctor-led hairline correction at our Uttara, Dhaka clinic as part of our hair transplant services. We study your face shape, forehead height, and donor density first, then design a hairline that suits you. The aim is a soft, natural result that looks like it was always yours.

Hairline correction services at HairTreat

Every hairline problem has a different cause and pattern. We recommend a plan only after examining your scalp and measuring the donor hair available.

Hairline lowering and forehead reduction

Grafts are placed along the front edge to bring a naturally high forehead down, giving the face better proportion without surgery that cuts the skin.

Receding hairline restoration

For men whose hair has pulled back at the front, grafts rebuild the lost frontal line and add density behind it so the area does not look thin.

Temple point and M-shaped hairline repair

Recession at the corners creates a deep "M" shape. We restore the temple points to bring the hairline back into a balanced frame.

Uneven or asymmetric hairline correction

If one side sits higher than the other, we level the line and match the angles so both sides look consistent.

Hairline correction after a previous transplant

A hairline that looks too straight, too low, too dense, or "pluggy" after an earlier procedure can often be softened and refined. Suitability depends on how much donor hair remains, and the doctor will tell you what is realistic.

Female hairline correction

Women with a naturally high forehead or a thinning front line can have a softer, rounded hairline designed for a feminine look. See also our female hair transplant treatment.

Hairline density improvement

When the line exists but looks sparse, extra grafts are placed to raise fullness and remove the see-through effect at the front.

Key points about hairline correction

A good hairline depends more on design than on the number of grafts. These are the principles behind our work.

  • Face-led design: The line is drawn to match your face shape, age, and forehead proportions, not copied from a template.
  • Irregular, soft edge: Natural hairlines are not straight. Single-hair grafts at the front create a gentle, uneven border that looks real.
  • Right graft in the right place: Single-hair grafts go at the edge, and thicker multi-hair grafts go behind them for density.
  • Natural angle and direction: Each graft is placed to match the way hair grows at the front of your scalp.
  • Planned for the long term: The hairline is designed with future hair loss in mind, so it still looks right as you age.
  • Minimal donor marks: FUE extraction leaves tiny dot-like marks that surrounding hair usually covers, with no long linear scar.

Who is a good candidate for hairline correction?

Not every hairline can be changed safely, which is why every case starts with an examination. If you are young and still losing hair, the doctor may advise stabilizing the hair loss first, because a lowered hairline can look out of place if the hair behind it keeps thinning. Hairline correction may suit you if you:

  • Have a high or wide forehead and want better facial balance
  • Have a receding hairline or deep temple recession
  • Have an uneven or asymmetric hairline
  • Are unhappy with the look of a previous hair transplant
  • Have enough healthy donor hair at the back and sides of the scalp
  • Are in good general health and have realistic expectations

Hairline correction recovery and results timeline

Healing differs from person to person, so treat this as a general guide, not a promise.

TimeframeWhat to expect
First 1 to 3 daysMild redness, tenderness, and possible swelling around the forehead. Avoid touching or scratching the grafts.
Days 7 to 10Small crusts loosen and fall away, and redness fades for most patients.
Weeks 2 to 4Transplanted hairs shed. This is normal, and the follicles stay in the skin.
Months 3 to 4New hair begins to appear, often fine at first.
Months 6 to 9Hair thickens, and the new hairline becomes more visible.
Months 12 to 15Final density and shape become clear.

Hairline correction cost in Bangladesh

Hairline correction cost in Bangladesh depends on how much needs to change. Lowering a hairline slightly takes far fewer grafts than rebuilding a deep recession or repairing a previous transplant. HairTreat gives a clear estimate after your consultation, so you know the graft count and total cost before you commit. You can also view our general hair transplant pricing.

What affects hairline correction cost:

  • Number of grafts: A small adjustment needs fewer follicles than a full frontal rebuild.
  • Size of the change: Lowering the line by a few millimeters, filling temples, and fixing asymmetry each need different amounts of work.
  • Technique used: Standard FUE usually costs less. Advanced options such as Sapphire FUE or DHI cost more because of the special tools and extra precision.
  • Donor hair quality: Limited or fine donor hair can slow extraction and affect planning.
  • Repair cases: Correcting an earlier transplant is more complex than a first procedure.
  • Clinic and aftercare: The doctor's experience, the clinic setup, and what is included (medicines, follow-ups, and aftercare) all affect the final price.

FAQ about hairline correction

Hairline correction is a procedure that reshapes or lowers the front hairline by moving healthy follicles from a donor area to the frontal scalp. It can fix a high forehead, receding temples, an uneven line, or a hairline that looks unnatural after a previous transplant.

Follicles taken from the back of the scalp are generally resistant to the factors that cause pattern baldness, so they usually keep growing in their new position. Results still depend on donor quality, healing, and following your aftercare instructions.

It varies widely. A modest adjustment may need around a thousand grafts or fewer, while a large frontal rebuild needs more. The doctor confirms the number after examining your scalp and discussing your goals.

The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so most patients feel only the initial injections. Mild soreness, tightness, or swelling can occur for a few days afterward.

Most sessions take several hours, depending on the number of grafts. Your doctor will give you a time estimate during planning.

Transplanted hairs usually shed within the first few weeks, which is normal. New growth often starts around months 3 to 4, density improves over the following months, and the final result is usually clear by 12 months or a little later.

Age alone is not the issue. If your hair loss is still progressing, the doctor may suggest stabilising it with medical treatment first, so the new hairline stays balanced over time.
