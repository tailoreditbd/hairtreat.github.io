Best beard transplant in Bangladesh | HairTreat

A patchy, thin, or scarred beard is hard to fix with grooming alone. Genetics, old skin conditions, and facial scars can leave gaps on the cheeks, chin, or jawline that no trimmer, oil, or serum can fill. A beard transplant in Bangladesh offers a lasting solution: healthy hair follicles are moved from a donor area, usually the back of the scalp, into the parts of the face where facial hair is thin or missing.

HairTreat provides doctor-led beard transplant treatment at our Uttara, Dhaka clinic as part of our hair transplant services. Before recommending anything, our team examines your facial hair pattern, donor density, and skin, then designs a beard line that suits your face shape and grooming style. The goal is an even, natural-looking beard and honest expectations, never an overdone or artificial look.

Beard transplant services at HairTreat

Every beard has its own pattern of gaps, growth direction, and density. HairTreat recommends a plan only after mapping the affected areas and checking how much donor hair is available.

Full beard transplant

Grafts are spread across the cheeks, chin, and jawline to build a complete, even beard for men with very light or uneven growth.

Patchy beard transplant

Small gaps and bare patches are filled so the new hair blends smoothly with the beard you already have.

Moustache transplant

A thin or incomplete moustache is shaped and thickened, with hairs angled downward and slightly outward to match natural growth.

Goatee and chin transplant

Density is added around the chin and below the lower lip for a defined goatee or a fuller chin line.

Sideburn and cheek line restoration

Grafts connect the sideburns to the beard and sharpen the upper cheek line for a cleaner outline.

Beard density improvement

When a beard grows but looks thin, extra grafts are placed between existing hairs to raise overall fullness.

Beard scar transplant

Selected patients with facial scars from acne, burns, injury, or surgery may receive grafts in the scarred skin. Scar tissue has a weaker blood supply, so growth can vary, and the doctor explains what is realistic before treatment begins.

Key points about beard transplant

Good results depend on planning as much as on the procedure itself. These are the principles behind our beard work.

  • Your own hair: Follicles come from your donor area, so the new beard grows as real hair and does not sit on the skin like a temporary product.
  • Natural direction and angle: Grafts follow the growth direction of each facial zone, so the beard flows the way natural facial hair does.
  • Soft edges, dense center: Single-hair grafts along the outline soften the borders, while multi-hair grafts add thickness where the beard is fullest.
  • Minimal donor-area marks: FUE extraction leaves tiny dot-like marks that surrounding scalp hair usually covers, with no long linear scar.
  • Easy to live with: Once healed, the transplanted beard can be washed, trimmed, and styled like the rest of your facial hair.

Who is a good candidate for a beard transplant?

Not everyone is suitable straight away. Limited donor hair, an active skin infection, or an uncontrolled medical condition may call for treatment first or a different plan. That is why every case starts with a proper examination. For many men in Bangladesh, a full beard is part of personal style, religious practice, or professional image. A beard transplant may suit you if you:

  • Have naturally sparse, patchy, or uneven beard growth
  • Have gaps caused by acne scars, burns, injuries, or previous surgery
  • Want a stronger mustache, goatee, sideburn, or cheek line
  • Have inactive, stable beard patches that your doctor has cleared for treatment
  • Have enough healthy donor hair at the back and sides of the scalp
  • Are in good general health and want realistic results

Beard transplant recovery and results timeline

Everyone heals at their own pace, so treat this timeline as a general guide rather than a promise.

Timeframe What to expect
First 1 to 3 days Mild redness, tenderness, and tiny crusts around the grafts. Avoid touching, rubbing, or scratching the area.
Days 7 to 10 Crusts loosen and fall away, and redness fades for most patients.
Weeks 2 to 4 Transplanted hairs shed. This is normal, and the follicles stay in the skin.
Months 3 to 4 New hair begins to appear, often fine and soft at first.
Months 6 to 8 The beard thickens and fills in more visibly.
Months 9 to 12 Final density and shape become clear.

Beard transplant cost in Bangladesh

Beard transplant cost in Bangladesh depends on the treatment area, the number of grafts, the technique used, and the level of aftercare support. A mustache repair needs far fewer grafts than a full beard, so the price changes with the size of the job. HairTreat provides a clear estimate after your consultation, so you know the graft count and total cost before you commit. You can also see our general hair transplant pricing for reference.

What affects beard transplant cost:

  • Number of grafts: The more follicles you need, the higher the cost. A mustache needs far fewer grafts than a full beard.
  • Area being treated: A full beard, patchy beard, goatee, sideburns, or cheek line each cover a different amount of skin and take a different amount of work.
  • Technique used: Standard FUE usually costs less. Advanced options like Sapphire FUE or DHI cost more because of the special tools and extra precision.
  • Donor hair quality: Thin or limited donor hair can make extraction slower and harder, which can change the price. Scar cases are also more complex.
  • Clinic and aftercare: The doctor's experience, the clinic setup, and what is included (medicines, follow-ups, aftercare kit) all affect the final price.
