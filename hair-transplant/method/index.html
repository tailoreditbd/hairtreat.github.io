Choosing a hair transplant is not only about the clinic. It is also about the method, because each technique collects and places hair in a slightly different way. The right match depends on your hair loss pattern, your donor hair, and the result you want.

Hair transplant methods at a glance

All five methods move healthy hair from the back and sides of your scalp to the thinning area. They differ mainly in how grafts are collected and placed, and the table below shows the key differences side by side.

Hair transplant methods provided by HairTreat

HairTreat offers FUE, Sapphire, Bio-Sapphire, DHI and FUT at its Uttara clinic in Dhaka. Here is what each method involves and who it usually suits.

FUE hair transplant

Follicular unit extraction takes healthy hair groups one at a time from the back and sides of your scalp, then implants them in the thinning area. It avoids a surgical strip, so the marks left behind are small and easy to hide under short hair.

Sapphire hair transplant

Sapphire uses the same graft collection as FUE, but the recipient channels are opened with a sapphire blade instead of a steel one. The blade is very smooth and fine, which helps the doctor shape small, well-angled channels for a soft hairline.

Bio-Sapphire hair transplant

Bio-Sapphire builds on the sapphire technique and adds supportive care aimed at the scalp and grafts during healing. It is meant for patients who want a more complete recovery plan on top of precise channel work. Your doctor will explain exactly what is included in your case.

DHI hair transplant

Direct hair implantation also collects grafts one by one, but each graft is loaded into a pen-like implanter and placed straight into the scalp. Because the channel and the graft go in together, the doctor can control depth, angle and spacing closely.

FUT hair transplant

Follicular unit transplantation removes a thin strip of scalp from the donor area. The strip is divided into individual grafts under magnification, and the wound is closed with fine stitches. It leaves a thin line scar, which can be covered by hair of a normal length.

How to choose the right method

A method is only a tool. The doctor's planning and the care taken with every graft decide how natural the result looks. No single method is best for everyone. During your consultation, the doctor looks at the following.

  • Stage and pattern of hair loss: A receding hairline, a thinning crown, and advanced baldness need different plans.
  • Donor hair strength: The number and quality of healthy hairs at the back and sides limit what can be moved.
  • Hair type and length: Thick, wavy, or coarse hair covers more area per graft than fine, straight hair.
  • Haircut preference: Short hair favors methods that leave only dot marks, while longer hair can hide a strip scar.
  • Budget and timeline: Sapphire and DHI usually cost more than standard FUE, and larger graft counts raise the total.
  • Health and expectations: Your medical history, lifestyle, and goals all shape a safe, realistic plan.

What every method has in common

Whichever method you choose, the basics of the procedure and the recovery journey follow the same path.

  • Grafts are taken from the permanent zone at the back and sides of the scalp.
  • Local anaesthesia keeps you comfortable during the procedure.
  • Transplanted hair sheds in the first weeks, then regrows from about month three or four.
  • Final results usually take 9 to 12 months, and sometimes longer.
  • Aftercare, such as gentle washing and avoiding scratching, matters as much as the surgery.

Beyond the standard methods

Hair transplant techniques can also be used for specific needs:

  • Female hair transplant for thinning and hairline concerns in women
  • Beard transplant for patchy or sparse facial hair
  • Hairline correction for results from an earlier procedure that need refining

What does each method cost?

The price of a hair transplant in Bangladesh depends on the method, the number of grafts, the area covered, and the aftercare included. Standard FUE is usually the more affordable starting point, while sapphire-based options and DHI generally cost more.
