DHI hair transplant in Bangladesh | HairTreat

Most hair transplants work in two stages: channels are opened in the scalp first, and the grafts are placed into them afterwards. DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) combines those two stages. Each graft is loaded into a fine, pen-like implanter and placed straight into the scalp, so the doctor controls the angle, depth, and spacing of every hair as it goes in. A DHI hair transplant in Bangladesh gives you that level of control close to home, without the cost of travel and without the worry of managing recovery from another country.

What is a DHI hair transplant?

DHI stands for Direct Hair Implantation. It is a variation of the follicular unit extraction (FUE) family of procedures. Healthy follicles are taken one at a time from the back and sides of the scalp, which is the permanent donor zone. What sets DHI apart is the implantation step. Instead of cutting channels first and filling them later, the doctor loads each graft into a hollow implanter pen and places it into the skin in a single movement.

This matters for a few practical reasons:

  • Controlled direction: The pen can be turned to match the natural growth angle of each zone, so the hair flows the way your own hair would.
  • Controlled depth: Grafts are set at a consistent depth, which supports an even, tidy surface.
  • Careful spacing: The doctor can place hairs between existing ones, which helps when the goal is to thicken a thinning area.
  • Less handling: Each graft goes from the pen to the scalp quickly, so it spends less time outside the body.

Hairline design and restoration

A receding or uneven front line is rebuilt with fine single-hair grafts placed at a soft, natural angle. Because the doctor decides the exact position of each hair, DHI suits detailed hairline work. If an earlier procedure left you unhappy with your hairline, see our hairline correction service.

Thinning areas with existing hair

Many people are not fully bald. They have weak, see-through areas where some native hair remains. The pen allows grafts to be placed between those existing hairs, so the doctor can add density without disturbing what is already growing.

Crown and vertex

Hair at the crown grows in a swirl. Grafts are placed to follow that pattern, which helps the area look full from every angle.

Eyebrow and beard areas

Small, defined areas need precise placement. DHI is often considered for these zones, where direction and spacing are very visible. Learn more on our eyebrow transplant and beard transplant pages.

Women's hair thinning

Women with diffuse thinning or a widening part may be candidates after a careful medical review. Our female hair transplant page explains how we assess this.

Key points about DHI hair transplant

A good result depends on planning as much as on the tool.

  • Your own hair: Follicles come from your donor zone, so the transplanted hair grows like natural hair and usually lasts.
  • No strip scar: Like other FUE-based methods, DHI leaves tiny dot-like marks in the donor area instead of a long line.
  • Close control of each graft: The doctor sets direction, angle, and depth during placement.
  • A safe graft number: The graft count is based on your donor capacity, not on a figure chosen to sound impressive.
  • Focused sessions: DHI is usually a careful, slower process, so it often suits targeted areas. Your doctor will tell you how much can be covered in one visit.

DHI vs. FUE: What is the difference?

DHI and FUE both take grafts one by one from your own donor hair, but they differ in how those grafts are placed: DHI places each one directly with an implanter pen, while FUE places grafts by hand into channels that are opened beforehand.

Who is a good candidate for DHI?

Not everyone is suitable straight away. An active scalp infection, very limited donor hair, or an uncontrolled medical condition may call for treatment first or a different plan. A DHI hair transplant may suit you if you:

  • Have a receding hairline or thinning at the front, top, or crown
  • Have weak, thinning areas where you want to add density between existing hair
  • Want to treat a defined area such as a hairline, eyebrow, or beard patch
  • Have enough healthy hair at the back and sides of the scalp
  • Are in good general health and want realistic results
  • Prefer to have your procedure and follow-up care in Bangladesh

Recovery and results timeline

Everyone heals at a different pace, so use this as a general guide, not a promise.

TimeframeWhat to expect
First 1 to 3 daysMild swelling, redness, and tiny scabs at the graft sites. Avoid rubbing or scratching.
Days 7 to 10Scabs loosen and fall away, and redness settles for most people.
Weeks 2 to 4Transplanted hairs shed. This is normal, and the follicles remain in the skin.
Months 3 to 4New hair starts to appear, often fine at first.
Months 6 to 8Hair thickens, and the added density becomes more visible.
Months 9 to 12 and beyondTexture and density keep improving. Some patients need up to 18 months to see the full result.

DHI hair transplant cost in Bangladesh

The cost of a DHI hair transplant in Bangladesh depends on the number of grafts, the area being treated, the quality of your donor hair, and what aftercare is included. DHI generally costs more than standard FUE because it uses specialized implanters and takes more time per graft. Because you are not paying for flights, hotels, or time away from work, the full cost is also easier to plan than it is for treatment abroad. For general reference, see our hair transplant pricing page.

What affects the cost:

  • Number of grafts: More follicles mean more extraction and placement time.
  • Area being treated: A hairline, a crown, and a dense-pack session each need a different plan.
  • Donor hair quality: Fine or limited donor hair can slow extraction and change the plan.
  • Technique and tools: DHI uses implanter pens and extra placement time, which usually raises the price compared with standard FUE.
  • Clinic and aftercare: The doctor's experience, the clinic setup, and what is included (medicines, follow-ups, and an aftercare kit) all affect the final figure.
