What is Turkish FUE hair transplant?

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) is a technique in which individual hair follicles are removed from the back and sides of the scalp and placed into thinning or bald areas. "Turkish FUE" is not a separate medical procedure. It is a popular name for the FUE style that Turkish clinics made well known: a high-graft session, a carefully drawn hairline, small-incision implantation, and a fixed treatment package.

At HairTreat, we apply the same principles with the same attention to detail:

  • Hairline first: The front line is drawn with your face shape, age, and future hair loss in mind.
  • Dense, layered placement: Single-hair grafts form the soft front edge, and multi-hair grafts build thickness behind it.
  • Careful extraction: Follicles are taken evenly from the donor area so it does not look thin or patchy.
  • One organized session: Most patients complete their treatment in a single day, depending on the graft count.

Key points about Turkish FUE hair transplant

Good results come from planning as much as from the procedure.

  • Your own hair: Follicles come from your donor area, so the transplanted hair grows like natural hair for the long term.
  • No long scar: FUE leaves tiny dot-like marks that surrounding hair usually covers, unlike a linear strip scar.
  • Natural direction and angle: Each graft follows the growth direction of its zone, so the hair flows naturally.
  • Realistic density: The doctor sets a safe graft number based on your donor capacity, not a number chosen to sound impressive.
  • Low downtime: Most people return to desk work within a few days, though early healing needs care.

Who is a good candidate?

Not everyone is suitable straight away. A weak donor area, an active scalp infection, or uncontrolled medical conditions may call for treatment first or a different plan. A Turkish FUE hair transplant may suit you if you:

  • Have male pattern baldness or a receding hairline
  • Have thinning at the crown or top of the scalp
  • Have enough healthy hair at the back and sides of the head
  • Are over about 25, when hair loss has become more predictable
  • Are in good general health and want realistic results
  • Prefer to avoid the cost and risk of traveling abroad for surgery

Recovery and results timeline

Everyone heals at their own pace, so treat this as a general guide, not a promise.

Timeframe What to expect
First 1 to 3 days Mild swelling, redness, and tiny scabs at the graft sites. Do not rub or scratch.
Days 7 to 10 Scabs loosen and fall away. Redness fades for most patients.
Weeks 2 to 4 Transplanted hairs shed. This is normal, and the follicles stay in the skin.
Months 3 to 4 New hair begins to appear, often fine at first.
Months 6 to 8 Hair thickens and the density becomes more visible.
Months 12 to 18 Final density and texture are clear.

Turkish FUE hair transplant cost in Bangladesh

The cost of a Turkish FUE hair transplant in Bangladesh depends on the number of grafts, the technique, your donor hair quality, and the aftercare included. Because you are not paying for international flights, a hotel stay, or time away from work, the total cost is usually easier to plan than it is for treatment abroad. After your consultation, HairTreat gives you a clear estimate, so you know the graft count and total price before you commit. See our general hair transplant pricing for reference.

What affects the cost:

  • Number of grafts: More follicles mean more extraction and implantation time.
  • Technique used: Standard FUE usually costs less. Sapphire FUE or DHI cost more because of the special tools and added precision.
  • Donor hair quality: Limited or fine donor hair can make extraction slower and may change the price.
  • Area being treated: A hairline needs a different plan from a full crown or an extensive bald area.
  • Clinic and aftercare: The doctor's experience, the clinic setup, and what is included (medicines, follow-ups, and aftercare kit) all affect the final price.
