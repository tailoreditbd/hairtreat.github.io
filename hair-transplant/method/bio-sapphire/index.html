Bio-sapphire hair transplant in Bangladesh

A transplant is only as good as the plan behind it and the health of the scalp it goes into. Many people in Bangladesh with a receding hairline, a thinning crown, or patchy density want more than extra grafts. They want grafts that settle well and grow into a natural-looking result.

Bio-sapphire hair transplant at HairTreat combines two things. The first is sapphire FUE, which uses fine sapphire blades to open the recipient channels. The second is biological scalp support in the form of PRP-based care. The aim is precise graft placement together with a healthier environment for the new follicles.

What is a bio-sapphire hair transplant?

Bio-sapphire is a sapphire FUE transplant with an added biological support step. Individual follicles are taken from the donor area, channels are opened with sapphire blades, and grafts are placed in the recipient area. Platelet-rich plasma (PRP), made from your own blood, supports the scalp during the procedure.

In simple terms:

  • Sapphire blades create small, clean channels with controlled angle and direction.
  • FUE extraction takes grafts one by one, so there is no linear strip scar.
  • PRP support adds your own growth factors to the treated scalp.
  • Your own hair is used, so the transplanted hair looks and feels natural.

Benefits of bio-sapphire hair transplant

Bio-sapphire combines precise sapphire channel creation with PRP scalp support, giving suitable patients a natural-looking hairline and a well-planned path to lasting density.

  • Natural hairline design with controlled graft direction
  • Fine channels that allow dense, careful placement
  • Your own PRP used as part of the plan
  • No linear donor scar, because grafts are extracted one by one
  • Works for the hairline, crown, and thinning areas
  • Permanent results from donor-area hair, with regular follow-up

Who is a good candidate?

It may not suit people with very limited donor hair, active scalp disease or unstable hair loss. In those cases the doctor may advise medical treatment first. See Hair PRP and GFC PRP for non-surgical options. Bio-sapphire may suit you if you:

  • Are an adult with stable or slowly progressing hair loss
  • Have a healthy donor area with enough grafts
  • Want hairline, crown or density improvement
  • Have thinning scalp areas that may benefit from extra support
  • Have realistic expectations about density and timing
