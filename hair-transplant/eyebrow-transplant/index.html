Why eyebrow transplant needs special skill

Eyebrows are small, but they are harder to design than most people expect. Eyebrow hair grows flat against the skin and changes direction across the brow. A few small mistakes in angle or density can make the result look unnatural. These are the principles behind our eyebrow work:

  • Your own hair: The follicles come from your donor area, so the new brow grows as real hair.
  • Correct angle and direction: Hairs near the head of the brow point upward, the body angles diagonally, and the tail runs almost flat. We follow this pattern in every graft.
  • Single-hair grafts: Fine, one-hair grafts give a soft edge, so the brow does not look blocky.
  • Tiny donor marks: FUE extraction leaves small dot-like marks that the surrounding scalp hair usually covers.
  • Easy upkeep: Scalp hair in the brow may grow longer than normal brow hair. A light trim now and then keeps the shape neat.

Who is a good candidate for an eyebrow transplant?

An eyebrow transplant is not right for everyone at the first visit. Limited donor hair, an active skin problem, or an unstable medical condition may need treatment first. That is why every case begins with a full examination. You may be a good candidate if you:

  • Have naturally thin or sparse eyebrows
  • Have lost brow hair from years of plucking, waxing, or threading
  • Have gaps caused by scars, burns, injury, or surgery
  • Are tired of daily pencil, powder, or tattoo touch-ups
  • Have enough healthy donor hair on the back or sides of the scalp
  • Are in good general health and want realistic results

Eyebrow transplant recovery and results timeline

Healing differs from person to person, so use this as a general guide, not a promise.

TimeframeWhat to expect
Days 1 to 3Mild redness and swelling, with tiny scabs around the grafts. Do not rub or scratch.
Days 7 to 10Scabs fall off and redness fades for most patients.
Weeks 2 to 4Transplanted hairs shed. This is normal, and the follicles stay under the skin.
Months 3 to 4New hairs start to appear, often fine at first.
Months 6 to 9Brows grow thicker and fuller.
Months 9 to 12Final density and shape become clear.

Eyebrow transplant cost in Bangladesh

The eyebrow transplant cost in Bangladesh depends on how many grafts you need, which part of the brow is treated, and the technique used. A light density top-up costs less than a full brow rebuild. After your consultation, HairTreat gives you a clear estimate with the graft count and total price before you decide. For general reference, see our hair transplant pricing.

What affects the cost:

  • Number of grafts: More grafts mean more time and a higher price.
  • Treatment area: One brow or both, a full rebuild or a partial fix.
  • Technique: Standard FUE usually costs less than advanced options such as Sapphire FUE.
  • Donor hair quality: Limited or thin donor hair can make extraction slower.
  • Clinic and aftercare: Doctor experience, clinic setup, and what is included, such as medicines and follow-ups.
