Our hair loss & restoration services

HairTreat treats hair loss across every stage and every part of the face and scalp. Here's what we offer under one roof:

Hair transplant methods

We match the technique to your scalp, not the other way around. We use various transplant methods such as FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) , Sapphire FUE , Bio-Sapphire, DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) , and FUT hair transplant .

Female hair transplant

Hair thinning in women often follows a different pattern than male pattern baldness. Our female-specific protocols address diffuse thinning without disrupting the natural hair flow.

Eyebrow transplant

Restores sparse or over-plucked eyebrows using fine, single-hair grafts placed at natural growth angles.

Beard transplant

Fills patchy or uneven beard growth with donor hair matched to your facial hair texture for a fuller, symmetrical look.

Hair line correction

Reshapes an unnatural, aggressive, or receded hairline into a softer, age-appropriate frame for the face.
